Market moves & Truth Social

Every significant move in the US indices since January 2024 — down and up — paired with the posts Donald Trump published while it happened. Click any event to inspect it.

These pairings are timing, not causation. Posts are surfaced because they landed in the window while the index was moving and mention market-relevant subjects. A high match score means "worth a look," never "this caused the move." This cuts both ways: a post before a rally is no more proof of cause than one before a selloff. Markets move on many things at once; judge each pairing yourself.
