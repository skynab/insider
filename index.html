Market dips & Truth Social

Every peak-to-trough drawdown in the US indices since January 2024, paired with the posts Donald Trump published during the slide. Click a dip to inspect it.

These pairings are timing, not causation. Posts are surfaced because they landed in the window while the index was falling and mention market-relevant subjects. A high match score means "worth a look," never "this caused the drop." Markets move on many things at once; judge each pairing yourself.
